Findings and actions
42.7% vs 2.8%

Contract length is the biggest lever

Month-to-month customers churn at 42.7%, one-year at 11.3%, two-year at 2.8%. Month-to-month is 55% of customers but 88.6% of all churn.

ActionOffer a discount or free add-on to move month-to-month customers to a 12-month plan, starting with those in their first year.

60.4% churn

Fiber on month-to-month, paying by e-check, is the riskiest group

1,307 customers in this group churn at 60.4%, more than twice the 26.5% average. Fiber overall churns at 41.9% against 19.0% for DSL.

ActionReview fiber pricing and service quality, and run a retention call for this group before month 6. The 966 retained month-to-month fiber customers bill $84.7K a month.

48.3% in year one

Churn is front-loaded

Customers in their first 11 months churn at 48.3%. After 48 months the rate is 9.6%. Churned customers also paid more per month: $74.40 against $61.30.

ActionBuild an onboarding check-in at months 2, 4 and 8, and review bills for new customers on high-priced plans.

41.6% vs 15.2%

Tech support customers stay

Among internet customers, those without tech support churn at 41.6% and those with it at 15.2%. Gender makes no real difference (26.9% female, 26.2% male).

ActionTrial free tech support for 3 months for at-risk fiber customers and compare churn against a control group. The gap shows association, so the trial is what proves cause.
